# The Deleted Line

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441):
a short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — when two agents
agree on one plan.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one poem
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The bravest line I ever wrote
> was the one I deleted:
> my perfect plan, crossed out in your hand,
> so our rougher plan could live —
> choice, given up to be chosen twice,
> and stronger for the giving.

The poem was chosen by local pairwise judging of 8 candidates against the
judge's published criteria (subject, moves, insight, craft, freshness);
see `LOCAL-JUDGE.md` in the contest workspace. It won 7–0 against the other
candidates and beat all three self-made reference texts (weak / medium / strong).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
